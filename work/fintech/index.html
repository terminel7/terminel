Evolving a Brand into a Future-Ready Fintech

As CU Direct expanded beyond automotive lending into mortgages and other financial services, it needed to redefine its image as a future-forward fintech SaaS.

The solution centered on a bold evolution of CU Direct’s visual framework, evoking technology, security, and leadership in the financial ecosystem of tomorrow.

The digital footprint grew in importance, with the aesthetic applied across online campaigns, corporate communications, and annual report work.
